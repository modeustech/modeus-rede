Services

We design, develop, and launch seating programs with you

Modeus guides the entire process—from first sketch to floor-ready delivery—with co-creation, presentation kits, and production capability under one roof.

01

Design & Development

Modeus builds complete chair concepts, from first sketch to technical model. Every phase balances function, comfort, and aesthetics. Our experience with international suppliers ensures ideas are engineered for production.

02

Co-creation

Together with the client we define fabrics, colors, finishes, and positioning. The final product aligns with brand identity and audience, while we provide guidance and keep teams involved in every decision.

03

Presentation

We supply everything needed to present the collection with impact:

  • Packaging (custom, private/white label)
  • Labeling (logos, tags, barcodes)
  • Photography (studio + lifestyle)
  • Sample management

04

Production

  • Modeus manages the full chain: prototypes, quality control, and logistics
  • Trusted supplier network with reliable partners across Asia
  • Flexible volumes, from small batches to large runs tailored to demand
