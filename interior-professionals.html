FAQ

Common questions from interior teams

These are the questions we most often get during sourcing cycles and after trade fairs.

How do I specify chairs for hospitality projects?

Start with the use case (dining, lounge, meeting, outdoor), then align on dimensions, finish requirements, and fabric suitability. We can share a spec pack and help you select configurations that match comfort, aesthetics, and durability needs.

Do you provide spec sheets and CAD files?

Yes—spec sheets are available, and CAD/project files can be provided on request depending on the model and configuration.

Can I request samples or fabric swatches?

Absolutely. For project work we recommend validating fabrics/finishes early, especially when multiple stakeholders are involved. Reach out and we’ll advise what to sample first.

What lead times should I plan for?

Lead time depends on whether you select a stocked option or a made-to-order configuration. Share your deadline and quantity range and we’ll advise a realistic plan from approval to delivery.

Do you support FF&E seating in Europe?

We support interior teams across Europe with seating concepts suited for hospitality and project environments—combining design, specification support, and production planning.

What’s the fastest way to shortlist options after a fair?

Send us your inspiration references (materials, colors, silhouettes) and project constraints (timeline, budget band, quantities). We’ll propose a shortlist and the next steps to validate specs and samples.
