About Modeus

Crafting seating for every space

We design and produce modular chairs that empower businesses to move from sketch to a successful rollout without a hitch.

Our story

Designed in Haarlem
Delivered across Europe

As a dedicated team, we focus entirely on creating durable chairs that fit seamlessly into any environment. Today, Modeus is a trusted partner for retailers and interior professionals across the Benelux and beyond.

Based in the Netherlands, we design every collection in-house. By combining internal expertise with trusted production partners, we ensure strict quality control and reliable delivery across every project.

Our expertise? Fully customizable modular seating. Configure the frame, seat, and upholstery to your liking—or choose from our in-stock best sellers for rapid delivery.
