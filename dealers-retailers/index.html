FAQ

Common questions from retailers

Short answers to the questions we get most often during sourcing and assortment planning.

Do you have chairs in stock for retailers?

Yes—our program includes stocked options designed for retail partners. Share what you’re looking for and we’ll propose a shortlist.

What retailer assets do you provide?

We can provide retailer-ready assets such as product photos, product information, and swatches to support webshop and showroom presentation.

How do I become a reseller / partner?

Request the retailer pack and tell us your channel mix (store, online, marketplace) and target customer. We’ll follow up with next steps and recommended models.

What are typical minimum order quantities?

MOQs depend on the model and the program (stock vs special requests). Share the quantities you need and we’ll advise the best fit.

Can I see new collections around IMM or Trendz?

Yes—if you’re planning around trade fairs, we can align a shortlist around your assortment plan and share what’s relevant for your customers.

What’s the fastest way to get products live on my webshop?

Start with a shortlist of stocked models, then use the retailer pack to prepare product pages and in-store presentation in parallel.
