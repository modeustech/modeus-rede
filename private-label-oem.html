Procurement intent

Visiting ORGATEC, Workspace Design Show, EquipHotel, or CIFF?

Procurement teams often shortlist OEM partners right after trade fairs—searching for exhibitors, contract seating suppliers, and private label capabilities. If you’re planning your next volume program, we can help translate your requirements into a brand-ready chair range.

A practical checklist

  • Before the fair: define target price band, positioning, must-have features, and timelines.
  • During the fair: collect reference models and finishes—then map them to workable OEM concepts.
  • After the fair: request an OEM brief template to align specs, customization, and rollout needs.

FAQ

Common questions from procurement teams

High-level answers to align scope, customization, and next steps without overpromising logistics, QC, or compliance guarantees.

What information do you need to quote an OEM chair program?

The fastest path is a clear brief: target market, price band, quantity range, timeline, reference styles, and any must-have materials or finishes. We’ll use that to propose a shortlist and next steps for sampling.

What can be customized on a chair program?

Typically upholstery, finishes, frame details, and branded elements. Share your brand requirements and we’ll outline what’s feasible for your timeline and intended use.

Do you support private label packaging and labeling?

Yes—packaging and labeling can be aligned to your brand presentation and rollout workflow.

What are typical MOQs for OEM programs?

MOQs depend on the model, materials, and how much customization is required. Once we review your brief, we’ll propose an approach that fits your volume goals.

What lead times should we plan for OEM production?

Lead times vary by specification and production planning. Share your deadline and we’ll align a realistic timeline for sampling, approval, and production.

Can you support hospitality / FF&E seating concepts?

Yes—we support project-oriented seating concepts and can tailor materials and finishes to the environment and usage requirements.
