Account Manager Retail (Benelux)

Haarlem · Full-time · Hybrid

Help retail and online partners grow our chair programs across the Benelux. You’ll manage a portfolio of resellers, plan launches, and keep assortments sharp.

In this role you build long-term relationships with physical stores and online platforms across the Benelux. You’ll visit key accounts, analyse what sells on the floor and online, and shape the right Jesper Home and Modeus mix per partner.

Together with our internal team you plan introductions, train store staff, and make sure our stocked and seasonal chairs are easy to sell. You are comfortable with numbers, simple reporting, and you enjoy spending time on the road.

We’re looking for someone who speaks Dutch and English, understands retail or e-commerce, and likes a mix of commercial and practical work.

Product Developer — Chairs

Haarlem · Full-time

Work with our suppliers and design team to translate ideas into durable, comfortable chairs ready for production and certification.

You translate sketches and moodboards into production-ready chair designs. You work closely with factories, test samples, and guard comfort, stability, and finish quality.

Your day-to-day includes setting up simple specs, following up on samples, and coordinating small changes for stocked and seasonal models. You’re comfortable working with technical drawings from partners and giving clear feedback.

Experience with furniture or product development is a big plus. You enjoy details, timelines, and working with international suppliers.

Content & Visual Merchandiser

Remote · Part-time / Freelance

Create product copy, simple landing pages, and visual guidelines that help our resellers tell a clear Jesper Home and Modeus story.

You turn product details into clear, friendly copy for product pages, lookbooks, and basic launch emails. You help translate our chair stories into simple guidelines resellers can follow.

You’ll work with photography, specs, and input from the commercial team to create text and simple content blocks. You understand what store teams and online shoppers need to see to make a decision.

Strong written English is required; Dutch or German is a plus. Experience with e-commerce, retail, or interior/furniture brands is helpful.
