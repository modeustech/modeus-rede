About Modeus

Our story

Designed in Haarlem
Shipped across Europe

We started as a small team with one aim: make well-built chairs for any and every space. Today, our Modeus furniture serve retailers and projects across the Benelux and Europe.

We’re based in the Netherlands, Haarlem. We design in-house and produce with trusted partners, with strict quality checks and reliable delivery.

Our specialty? Modular, highly customizable seating. Configure the frame, seat, fabric, and finish—or choose from in-stock best sellers for speed.
